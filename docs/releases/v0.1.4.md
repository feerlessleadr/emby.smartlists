## v0.1.4

**New: Most Recently Watched Round Robin sort**

A new sort, "Most Recently Watched Round Robin (Interleave)", orders shows by the one you watched most recently (never-watched shows last). Any playback counts, including an unfinished episode.

Combined with the rule `Next Unwatched = true` and Group By Series Name, it gives a "Next Up" playlist: one episode per show you are in the middle of, the show you watched last first. Fully watched shows (for example ones you rewatch) never appear.

Upgrade note: replace the single `Emby.Plugin.SmartLists.dll` and restart Emby. Existing lists are not changed.

The Continue Watching template now excludes shows you have never started. For your own Next Up playlist, set **Include unwatched series** to **No** on the Next Unwatched rule.
